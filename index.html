01 / About

Engineer at heart

I describe myself as someone who's naturally curious, I love solving complex problems and I'm passionate about creating impact! I'm an alumni of BITS Pilani and now writing backend code full time. Since July 2024 I've been an SDE II at Tata Communications, working across an audit logging microservice, a managed vector database platform, and the big data stack sitting under both.

The problems I enjoy are the ones where correctness and throughput pull against each other. Ordering lifecycle events streaming in from 100+ clusters without losing any. Getting 5x more out of a RabbitMQ consumer without breaking sequence. Knowing an alert fired before a customer had to tell us.

Before this I spent a year at Uber on the CRM data side, auditing warehouse tables and shipping pipelines. That year taught me something I still use: a dataset nobody trusts is more expensive than no dataset at all.
